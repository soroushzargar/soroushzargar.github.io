About

Deep learning models are confident, persuasive, and frequently wrong. My research builds the scaffolding that surrounds them — efficient algorithms with provable, distribution-free guarantees on uncertainty, coverage, and robustness.

I am a PhD researcher in Trustworthy AI, advised by Prof. Dr. Aleksandar Bojchevski at CISPA Helmholtz Center for Information Security and the University of Cologne. My work centers on conformal prediction, uncertainty quantification, and adversarial robustness — with a current focus on graph-structured data and large language models.

Before academia, I shipped production ML systems: backend services for quantitative trading and electricity-market forecasting at national scale. More recently I finetuned LLMs for speech translation at Apple, and an Applied Scientist internship at Microsoft is planned for 2026.
