About

My research centers at making trustworthy statistical guarantees on machine learning behavior cheaper to use. That umbrella covers robustness, and uncertainty quantification.

I am a post-doctoral researcher in the research group of Dr. Rebekka Burkholz at CISPA, Germany. I also had the experience of working in industry research at Apple.

Before that I was a PhD student focused on Trustworthy AI, advised by Prof. Dr. Aleksandar Bojchevski at CISPA, and University of Cologne. My work centered on conformal prediction, uncertainty quantification, and adversarial robustness — with a current focus on graph-structured data and large language models.

Research

Most of what I work on fits in two small models. Both run live in this page, so change the numbers and see what the guarantees do.

A set of labels instead of one guess. Conformal prediction returns a set that contains the true label with a probability you choose. Move the point: the set grows where the classes overlap. Then give an attacker a radius ε. The standard guarantee breaks, and robust sets pay in size to keep it.

Neighbours know something. In many graphs, connected nodes tend to share a label. Mixing each node’s scores with its neighbours’ keeps the coverage where you asked for it and makes the sets smaller. Raise λ and watch the nodes darken.
